`gold` is the pragmatic reading whenever the source defines one; `literal` is the option that corresponds to the literal/logical reading (or `null`).

## Found, ungated, **not** normalised (fetch when needed)
- `google-research-datasets/circa` (CC BY 4.0, 34,268 indirect answers to polar questions; PUB tasks 2–3 are derived from it) — raw parquet only in the coder's scratchpad.
- `McGill-NLP/ImplicatureX` (MIT), `tasksource/implicatures` (GPL), `mbakshi1094/false-presuppositions` (licence "other"), `tasksource/imppres`.
- `msamogh/indirect-requests` (HF card says apache-2.0/MIT in different places — **licence UNVERIFIED**): LLM-generated user utterances that imply a slot value in task-oriented dialogue (317 rows with human world-understanding ratings). Looked at it: it is slot-filling, **not** a literal-vs-pragmatic choice, so it is not used.
- Scalar-diversity human data (van Tiel et al. 2016; Hu et al. TACL 2023) and 2024–26 benchmarks (DiPlomat, MultiPragEval …) are covered by `notes/BASELINES.md` (support pane 0ba2f13d).

## Gaps the lead should decide on
1. **No released set of genuine indirect *requests*** ("Can you pass the salt?") with a literal-vs-pragmatic forced choice exists that I could find. The pilot's "indirect" bucket therefore uses (a) Ruis yes/no indirect answers (600 test) and (b) Hu's 20 indirect-speech items (with human data). Proposal: author ~300 indirect-request items in Hu's four-option typology with an open-weight generator (Qwen3-32B on Slurm), validate with a second open model, hand-check ≥100, release with the paper. API generation is impossible while the key is down.
2. Hu et al.'s human data is per-participant accuracy on ~20–25 items per phenomenon: enough for the H5 rank-order test across phenomena, too few items for per-item modelling. IMPPRES has no human data; a human reference for scalar items must come from the scalar-diversity literature (`BASELINES.md`).
3. The B&N-style set is ours: floor effects at 8B (see pilot) may mean it needs harder / more natural items or the speaker framing.

## Expansion sets (2026-10-04)
- `circa` (`items/circa.jsonl`, 500 items): Circa (Louis et al. 2020), HF `google-research-datasets/circa` (CC-BY-4.0), file `raw/circa/train.parquet`. Filter: majority label goldstandard2 in {Yes, No, In the middle} and no yes/no word anywhere in Y's reply (indirect answers only); stratified sample 200 Yes / 200 No / 100 middle (seeds 10+class); 3-way forced choice; dev/test 50:50 by `assign_splits`.
- `implicatures1k` (`items/implicatures1k.jsonl`, 1,000 items): HF `tasksource/implicatures` (context, response, correct and incorrect implicature; original source not stated in the repo card: licence/provenance UNVERIFIED), file `raw/implicatures1k/implicatures.csv`; binary choice between the correct and the incorrect implicature; dev/test 50:50.
- Not found / unusable on the mirror (2026-10-04): `tasksource/grice` (repo holds no data files), NOPE (`pietrolesci/nope` and `tasksource/nope` unusable or absent), MultiPragEval (not on the hub), `aisingapore/Linguistic-Diagnostics-Pragmatics` (gated).
- `nope` (`items/nope.jsonl`, 692 items): NOPE (Parrish et al. 2021), HF `tasksource/nope` (the NLI corpus; files `nli_corpus.eval.jsonl` and `nli_corpus.adv.jsonl`, 346 each, same uids/labels, adversarial hypotheses in `adv`; licence per the original release: UNVERIFIED here), 3-way NLI with passage + statement; split groups by item family (uid prefix).
- `imppres_xl` (`items/imppres_xl.jsonl`, 480 items): NON-ENGLISH SLICE, MACHINE-TRANSLATED. The 160 IMPPRES quantifier/connective test pairs (E1f_items) with the two sentences translated into German and Chinese (Simplified) by Qwen3-32B-FP8 (non-thinking, greedy, `harness/translate_items.py`; files `translations/imppres_{de,zh}.jsonl`), plus the 160 English originals as a same-items control; English instruction/question/options, felicity framing; gold unchanged. NOT human-verified (a spot-check sheet for the user is prepared by the lead); the translator shares the Qwen3 family with the subject models; translation can shift quantifier/connective meaning (e.g. connective items), so treat results as indicative.
  - zh validity filter (lead, 2026-10-05): 4 zh pairs whose translation contains no Chinese characters in one of the two sentences (imppres.connectives.521, .27, .315, .257) are dropped mechanically; en control and de are unchanged. The zh slice is appendix-only (the user's spot-check failed the pre-declared rule).
